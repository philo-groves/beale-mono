# Fleet vendor marks

The Apple, Windows, and Tux SVGs are from [Bootstrap Icons](https://github.com/twbs/icons), licensed under the MIT license in [LICENSE](LICENSE). They are used as vector marks in Fleet cards; the Apple and Windows marks are also reused in Plugin cards.
