# Fleet browser logos

- Brave, Chrome, Edge, Firefox, Opera, and Safari: 64 × 64 PNGs from [Browser Logos](https://github.com/alrra/browser-logos/tree/main/src). Source files are `src/<name>/<name>_64x64.png`.
- DuckDuckGo: `192.png` from the [DuckDuckGo Help Pages](https://github.com/duckduckgo/duckduckgo-help-pages/blob/master/192.png), resized to 64 × 64 at runtime.

Browser Logos licenses its project material under MIT and notes that logos and trademarks belong to their owners. The DuckDuckGo image is an official DuckDuckGo asset. These marks identify the installed browsers in Fleet.
